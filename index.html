Our Story

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Nightingale International Secondary School & College has proudly served the families of Kupondole, Lalitpur and the wider Kathmandu Valley since 1992, with more than 10,100 students having passed through our doors across three decades.

Our motto — LABORE ET HONOR (Through Work Comes Honour) — is the spirit that drives every student, every teacher, and every family. Excellence is built through consistent effort and moral integrity.

From PlayGroup through +2 College streams in Science, Management, and Law, we provide a complete educational journey under one roof.
